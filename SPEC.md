# Design Specification: Animals Playing in Nature

## Concept & Style
A bright, cheerful vector illustration featuring wild animals (a bear, deer, fox, and rabbit) playing together in an open nature setting. The artwork adopts the color palette and composition of the reference image—vibrant sky blues, fluffy white clouds, and lush green rolling hills.

## Composition
- **Sky & Background**: Clear blue gradient sky with light clouds and birds flying overhead.
- **Midground**: Gentle rolling green hills with a signature solitary leafy tree and background pine trees.
- **Foreground**: Sunny grass meadow adorned with wildflowers, butterflies, and four friendly animals interacting playfully.

## Color Palette
- **Sky**: `#1268C3`, `#258CF4`, `#62B4FF`
- **Hills/Grass**: `#558B2F`, `#689F38`, `#7CB342`, `#8BC34A`, `#9CCC65`
- **Bear**: `#6D4C41`, `#D7CCC8`
- **Deer**: `#A1887F`, `#8D6E63`
- **Fox**: `#EF6C00`, `#E65100`
- **Rabbit**: `#ECEFF1`, `#FFCDD2`

## Checklist Compliance
- Matched style and color tones from the reference image (vibrant blue sky and green hills).
- Included several animals playing in the meadow.
- No text inside the image graphic.
